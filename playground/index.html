Design a group of agents and see where it lands on the equal-budget ladder

Every option below is a component we measured in the paper. Pick what the agents see, what they exchange and how they organize. The page shows the group's accuracy, its token budget in independent agents (nB), pass@nB of independent agents at that budget, the margin, the ladder level, and how information moves. Options we did not measure are greyed out.

Levels: 1 = outperforms a single agent, 2 = outperforms the majority vote of nB independent agents, 3 = exceeds their pass@nB (one-sided paired tests at 0.025, clustered by question). pass@nB is shown as accuracy minus margin. "Not reported" marks quantities the paper does not give for that cell.
